Privacy Policy

Sign PDF Documents (Chrome extension). Last updated: October 29, 2026

Sign PDF Documents lets you add signatures, initials, dates, text and checkmarks to PDF files and download a signed copy. This policy explains what data the extension handles. In short: your documents and signatures stay on your device.

Documents

PDF files are opened, edited and saved entirely inside an extension tab in your browser. The extension never uploads your documents to us or to any third party, and we have no servers that receive them.

If you use “Sign this PDF” on a link or a PDF page, the extension downloads that file directly from the website where it is hosted, the same way your browser would when you open the link. If that website requires it, the extension asks you for access to that one site; you can decline, and you can remove this access at any time on the extension’s details page.

Signatures and settings

Signatures and initials you choose to save, and your preferred date format, are stored locally with Chrome storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. They are not synced or sent anywhere. You can delete a saved signature in the editor, or remove all data by uninstalling the extension.

What we do not collect

The extension does not collect personal information, browsing history, analytics or usage statistics, and it does not use cookies or tracking. It contains no ads and does not sell or share any data. All code is included in the extension package; no remote code is loaded.

Permissions

  • storage – save your signatures and settings on your device.
  • contextMenus – add the “Sign this PDF” item to the right-click menu.
  • Optional site access – requested only for the single site of a PDF you open with “Sign this PDF”, and only when that site does not allow the file to be downloaded otherwise.

Website pages

The welcome and uninstall pages of this extension may use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Umami) to count visits. These pages never receive your documents or signatures. If you answer the optional question on the uninstall page, only your answer is stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
